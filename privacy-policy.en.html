Brizz Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Brizz is a calming app for children, published by 8DConcept. This page explains, plainly, what the app does — and does not — do with your data.

In short

Brizz collects no personal data. No account is required. The app runs entirely on the device, with no connection to a server, and nothing is ever sent to 8DConcept or any third party.

Microphone

Two exercises (bubbles and clouds) react to the child's breath. To do this, the app measures the sound level captured by the microphone, live, on the device. This processing is instant and local: no audio is ever recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. If microphone access is denied, both exercises still work by holding a finger down instead.

Settings and progress

Preferences (enabled techniques, sound volume, unlocked techniques) are stored only on the device. They are never synced, backed up online, or accessible to 8DConcept.

Purchases

Unlocking the advanced techniques uses a single one-time in-app purchase, handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store depending on your device. Brizz never sees or stores any payment information.

Ads and tracking

Brizz shows no ads and includes no tracking, behavioral analytics, or audience measurement tools.

Children

Brizz is designed to be used by children, with settings controlled by a parent or responsible adult. Since no data is collected at all, no information about a child is ever gathered, stored, or shared.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

Contact
